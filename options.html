How the toll works

  • Each word above a book’s highest recorded total earns one credit. Deleting and restoring words earns credits once.
  • A new book starts at its existing word count with zero earned credits. Open each book now to establish its starting point.
  • Phone writing is credited when WritingHabit next shows the updated Overall total on this desktop. Reload the chapter after phone edits if needed.
  • One credit buys one minute. Time is measured continuously and partial minutes carry over across tabs and restarts.
  • Only a listed site in Brave’s focused tab spends credits. Background tabs, other apps, minimized windows, and a locked computer pause the toll. Reading and watching videos in a focused tab still count.
  • The toolbar turns amber while spending. Large balances are abbreviated in the badge. Hover or open the popup for the full balance.
